Can LLMs Beat Classical Hyperparameter Optimization Algorithms?

A Study on autoresearch

Fabio Ferreira, Lucca Wobbe, Arjun Krishnakumar, Frank Hutter, Arber Zela

Paper (arXiv) Code (GitHub)
TL;DR: We benchmark 9 HPO methods — classical, LLM-based, and hybrid — on Karpathy's autoresearch task under identical 24-hour budgets with 3 seeds. Classical methods (CMA-ES, TPE, SMAC) consistently outperform pure LLM-based agents within a fixed search space. Even frontier models like Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview do not close the gap. Our hybrid method Centaur, which shares CMA-ES's full internal state (mean, step-size, covariance matrix) with the LLM, achieves the best result in our experiments.
